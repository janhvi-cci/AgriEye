How it works

From bare field to advisory, in four steps.

No hardware, no site visit required to get started — just a boundary, and the next satellite pass does the rest.

1

Register your field

Farmers, dealers, or cooperative reps mark a field boundary and a few basics — crop, size, location. Takes under five minutes. Register your field →

2

We scan it from orbit

Free Sentinel-1 and -2 passes image the field every few days; our pipeline handles cloud masking and vegetation indices automatically.

3

Get a plain-language advisory

NDVI trends, soil moisture, and stress signals turn into a fertilizer, irrigation, or pest-control recommendation — sent by SMS, WhatsApp, or IVR.

4

Track the season

Every pass adds to the field's record — building the crop-health history that lenders, insurers, and carbon buyers can act on.
